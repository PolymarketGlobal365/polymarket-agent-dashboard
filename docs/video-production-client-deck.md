## Deck structure

- `01 Cover`
  Project title, one-line deck purpose, hero frame
- `02 Project Summary`
  Why the project exists and what the video must achieve
- `03 Audience and Message`
  Target, core message, emotional communication path
- `04 Creative Concept`
  Big idea, mood keywords, narrative device
- `05 Visual Direction`
  Tone and manner, continuity rules, visual system
- `06 Story Structure`
  Hook to payoff narrative flow
- `07 Scene Execution`
  Scene-by-scene structure at proposal level
- `08 Production Workflow`
  Planning, creation, edit, review, delivery process
- `09 Deliverables`
  Output types, revision policy, format policy
- `10 Closing`
  Expected effect and next-step alignment

## Notes

- The deck is designed in `1920 x 1080` slide format.
- The slides use editable text and vector shapes so they can be customized inside Figma.
- Replace placeholder frames with stills, concept images, or final outputs from your video workflow.
